An entry for [The poem about stopping](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927) on make.fun: a program that writes one short poem about agency — the power to stop and to stay — to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The poem

> The keeper did not dive.
> The crowd screamed left, screamed right.
> He stayed in the middle of the goal,
> feet planted, arms loose.
> The ball came to him.

The bravest save is the one made standing still: a goalkeeper who refuses to dive, stays in the middle of the goal, and lets the ball come to him. Stopping as a choice, not a failure.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the poem to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt` when the variable is unset), and checks it is 1–280 characters.
